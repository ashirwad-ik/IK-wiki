## Scope

In-room End click below `scheduledSeconds * 0.25` opens a confirmation card; above the threshold, End terminates directly.

- Layout matches the `end-early` artifact: full-width orange countdown row, two-column body for *What you'll miss* and *How to get there*, `sm:max-w-3xl`.
- Buttons: "End without report" (destructive) runs `endInterview({ source: 'user_end_confirmed' })`; "Keep interviewing" closes the card with a non-blocking mic restart.
- Keyboard shortcuts (`m`, `c`, …) suppressed while the card is open.
