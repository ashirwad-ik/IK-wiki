## Scope

Show the "Before you go" card exactly once per interview when focus leaves the interview surface — tab-switch, window-blur, OS-switch, cursor-to-tab-bar. Does **not** fire on the native "Leave site?" prompt or its Cancel.

- New hook `src/hooks/useFocusLeaveOnce.ts` — fires at most once per mount on `visibilitychange → hidden` or `document.mouseleave` (`clientY ≤ 0`, debounced 450 ms).
- `beforeunload` + `mouseenter` cancel any pending mouseleave trigger so the card never queues behind the native prompt.
- `window.blur` deliberately NOT listened to (Chrome fires blur on native prompt open; listening would re-introduce the Cancel-shows-card regression). JSDoc in the hook documents this.
- Reuses `BeforeYouGoDialog`. Stay restores mic; End runs standard end-interview flow.
